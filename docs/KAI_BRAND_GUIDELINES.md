# KaiMS brand guidelines

KaiMS is calm, technical, trustworthy and enterprise-grade. Use Kai Midnight,
Intelligence Blue, Signal Cyan and AI Violet for identity. Red is reserved for
critical, failed, danger and blocked states. Operational meaning must always
include text or an icon in addition to color.

Use **Kai Insight**, **Kai Evidence**, **Kai Confidence**, **Kai Risk** and
**Kai Decision** for intelligence primitives. Datamatics appears only through
the restrained endorsement “by Datamatics”.
