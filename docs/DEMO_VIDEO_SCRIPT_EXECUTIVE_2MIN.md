# KaiMS Executive Demo Video Script (2 Minutes)

Audience: Executive leadership
Length: 2 minutes
Goal: Show business value, governance, and outcomes fast.

## 0:00 - 0:20 Problem and Promise

On screen:
- KaiMS UI landing view with key tabs visible.

Voiceover:
- "Incidents are expensive when teams switch tools and lose context. KaiMS unifies alert intake, AI-assisted diagnosis, and governed remediation in one operational workflow."

## 0:20 - 0:45 Incident to Decision

On screen:
- Alerts and Quick Docs tab.
- Select a high-severity alert.
- Trigger workflow.

Voiceover:
- "From the moment an alert arrives, KaiMS routes it through a multi-agent decision pipeline that identifies probable root cause and recommended action."

## 0:45 - 1:10 Governance and Risk Control

On screen:
- Agent Flow tab with role status.
- Approval state shown for HIGH or CRITICAL.
- Gateway Safety tab.

Voiceover:
- "For high-risk incidents, human approval is enforced before execution. Gateway safety policies and trace IDs provide audit-ready governance."

## 1:10 - 1:35 Cost and Operational Transparency

On screen:
- FinOps tab with model usage and cost details.

Voiceover:
- "Every model interaction is measured for usage and cost, giving leaders full visibility into AI spend and operational efficiency."

## 1:35 - 2:00 Closure and Business Outcome

On screen:
- Closed Incidents tab.
- Closure report and lessons learned.

Voiceover:
- "KaiMS validates closure and converts incidents into reusable knowledge. The result is faster recovery, lower operational risk, and measurable governance from alert to closure."

## Executive One-Line Close

- "KaiMS turns incident response from reactive firefighting into governed, measurable, and repeatable operations."

## Recording Notes

- Use one consistent theme mode throughout.
- Keep cursor movement deliberate.
- Focus on outcomes, not internal implementation details.
